Telkom Kenya · Regulatory Transformation

National KYC compliance in 6 months — or decommission the network

Situation

The Kenyan government mandated 100% KYC compliance within six months. Telkom had 5M+ customers at ~40% compliance versus a 70%+ industry average. Non-compliance meant decommissioning lines — a >50% revenue loss and possible company collapse.

Approach

Ran SQL audits and field ethnographies to map every gap, split delivery into two chartered workstreams (existing base + new sign-ups), built a self-service web form, digitized paper databases, phased service denial, culled fraud-prone USSD channels, and deployed OCR with direct government database validation — with compliance-linked agent commissions.

Results

  • New registration compliance: 13% → 100%
  • Existing base compliance: >70% before exit
  • Zero loopholes in a 10k/day agent channel
  • Company decommissioning risk avoided

KOKO Networks · Payments Infrastructure

PII masking rescue: keeping 80% of company revenue alive in under 4 weeks

Situation

In my second month as Principal PM, Safaricom announced PII masking in their payment API. With 100% of customer payments via mobile money, failure to identify payers would zero out wallet top-ups and purchases. Timeline: 30 days. Kenya = 80%+ of company revenue.

Approach

Co-designed a hashing algorithm with Safaricom, generated hash digests for the entire 1.2M-customer database as the new payment identifier, auto-created digests at registration, decommissioned the risky "pay-first" flow, built an SLA-bound reversal process for unrecognized payments, and updated all process documentation and the care playbook.

Results

  • Delivered end-to-end in <4 weeks, ahead of deadline
  • Zero payment interruptions during cutover
  • Plugged a major data-privacy legal exposure (Kenya & Rwanda)
  • 1.2M customer records migrated seamlessly

From post-school tech training to a working-professional upskilling business

KOKO Networks · Growth Strategy

Are milk ATMs a viable business? A two-week answer to a board-level question

Situation

KOKO operated a fast-growing network of smart bio-ethanol dispensing "ATMs" across Kenya and Rwanda. Leadership saw an ARPU growth opportunity in milk vending through the same route-to-market — but needed a defensible answer fast.

Approach

Ran consumer research and value-chain analysis, sized the market, assessed strategic fit with the existing network and proprietary IP, modeled delivery options up to gross-margin level, and pressure-tested the idea in a strategy paper.

Results — 2 weeks

  • Full commercial viability assessment & strategy paper
  • Market sizing + value-chain analysis
  • Gross-margin viability models for delivery options
  • Board-ready findings presentation
